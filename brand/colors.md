# VadeCom Colour Palette


> **اعتماد المالك 2026-09-26:** هذه اللوحة هي ألوان فاديكوم في كل مكان (الموقع، المدونة، بانرات المقالات، السوشيال، العروض، الإعلانات، وكل ما تولّده أدوات الذكاء الاصطناعي). العمل داخلها قدر الإمكان وعدم الخروج عليها؛ أي احتياج للتباين يُحل باللوح الأبيض 10% والخط الرفيع على الأزرق، أو الأبيض والخط الأزرق 12% على الفاتح، لا بدرجة جديدة.

This file is the single authority for colour in every VadeCom asset: web, social,
print, decks, proposals, ads, motion, and anything generated by an AI tool.
If another file, template, brand kit or design disagrees with this file, that
other thing is wrong and is to be fixed.

## 1. The palette is a closed set

Only the eight values below may appear in a VadeCom asset. Any colour not listed
here is **prohibited**.

- No "close enough" values. `#0060B0` is not `#005CB4`. [off-palette]
- No eyedropper sampling from existing designs, screenshots, logos or reference
  images. Existing work is measured against this file, never the reverse.
- No new hues, tints or shades. The permitted derivations are **opacity steps
  of `#005CB4`**, **three white overlays on blue-base**, and **two green tints**
  inside success components (see §3).
- Colours from earlier guides that are not in this table are prohibited,
  including `#FF6B35` (orange). [off-palette]
- The three brand colours are **blue, red and yellow**. Everything else in the
  table is a text, surface or functional colour.
- `green-success` is **functional**, not a brand colour: it marks positive
  states only and never carries the brand.

| Token           | Hex       | RGB           | Role                                   |
|-----------------|-----------|---------------|----------------------------------------|
| `blue-base`     | `#005CB4` | 0, 92, 180    | **Primary.** The VadeCom blue.         |
| `red-accent`    | `#C9002B` | 201, 0, 43    | Accent only (on light surfaces).       |
| `yellow-cta`    | `#FFC71B` | 255, 199, 27  | CTA buttons; accent on blue-base.      |
| `green-success` | `#008F4C` | 0, 143, 76    | Functional: ticks, pricing, comparisons.|
| `navy-ink`      | `#001A3D` | 0, 26, 61     | Text only.                             |
| `surface-white` | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Background, cards, panels.             |
| `surface-soft`  | `#F5F7FA` | 245, 247, 250 | Background.                            |
| `grey-500`      | `#8A94A6` | 138, 148, 166 | Secondary, non-text and large-text use.|

## 2. Rules per colour

### `blue-base` — `#005CB4`
- The primary colour. Fills, CTAs (buttons, links), key graphics, charts' first
  series, and icon fills.
- **The only approved coloured background.** A slide, section, card or social
  post that is not white or soft is blue-base.
- Text on blue-base is `surface-white` only. Never `navy-ink`, `red-accent` or
  `grey-500` on blue-base.

### `red-accent` — `#C9002B`
- Accent only. Approved uses: **one** emphasised word per headline, underlines,
  alert/notification dots, a single highlighted number.
- Stays under **~5% of the canvas**. Never a background, never a fill for large
  shapes, never body text, never more than one emphasised word per headline.
- Red never sits on blue-base (they do not contrast: 1.10:1). On blue-base,
  the accent role passes to `yellow-cta`.

### `yellow-cta` — `#FFC71B`
- **CTA buttons** ("Get a quote", "Book a consultation", "Contact us") on
  any surface. One primary CTA per screen, slide or post.
- Button label is `navy-ink` only. Never white on yellow (1.56:1).
- **Accent on blue-base:** one emphasised word or number in a headline on a
  blue background, large text only (4.21:1 passes for 24px+ / 18.66px+ bold).
- Never a background, never body text, never a large shape, never text on
  white or soft surfaces (1.56:1). On light surfaces the accent is
  `red-accent`; yellow appears there only as a button fill.
- Red and yellow never appear as accents in the same headline.

### `green-success` — `#008F4C`
- Functional only: ✓ tick marks, "included" items in pricing, the positive
  side of before/after comparisons, success/confirmation messages, the final
  "result" step of a process.
- On `surface-white` / `surface-soft` only (4.17:1): icons and ticks at any
  size; text only when large (24px+, or 18.66px+ bold). Never body text.
- White text on a green fill (e.g. a step circle) is large-text only.
- Never on blue-base (1.58:1), never a page/section background, never a
  button, never in headlines as a brand accent, never in logos or social
  brand graphics.
- Tints allowed inside success components only: `green-08`
  `rgba(0,143,76,.08)` background and `green-25` `rgba(0,143,76,.25)` border.

### `navy-ink` — `#001A3D`
- **Text only.** Headlines and body copy on `surface-white` and `surface-soft`.
- **Never a background**, never a fill, never a large shape, never a gradient
  stop. A "dark" section uses `blue-base`, not navy.

### `surface-white` — `#FFFFFF`
- Default background. Cards, panels, and text on `blue-base`.

### `surface-soft` — `#F5F7FA`
- Secondary background for alternating sections, table stripes, input fields
  and content wells. Never as text.

### `grey-500` — `#8A94A6`
- Secondary uses: dividers, borders, disabled states, icon strokes,
  placeholder text, captions and metadata rendered as large text
  (24px+ regular, or 18.66px+ bold).
- **Not for body text or headlines.** Grey-500 on white is 3.06:1, which fails
  WCAG AA for normal-size text. Body copy is `navy-ink`.
- Never a background, never a fill for large shapes.

## 3. Opacity steps of `blue-base`

- `blue-base` may be used at any opacity over `surface-white` or `surface-soft`
  (backgrounds of sections, hover states, chart fills, subtle tints).
- Declare the step as `#005CB4` **with an alpha** — `rgba(0, 92, 180, 0.12)`,
  `#005CB41F`, "blue-base 12%". Do not flatten it to a new opaque hex:
  what 12% blue-base looks like on white is not a palette colour. [off-palette: `#E5EFF7`]
- Opacity steps are for `blue-base` only. No opacity steps of red, navy,
  yellow or grey.

### White overlays on `blue-base`
On a `blue-base` background only, white may be used at three fixed opacities:

| Token              | Value                    | Use                                  |
|--------------------|--------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| `on-blue-text2`    | `rgba(255,255,255,.85)`  | Secondary text on blue (minimum .85) |
| `on-blue-panel`    | `rgba(255,255,255,.10)`  | Cards, panels, secondary buttons     |
| `on-blue-border`   | `rgba(255,255,255,.22)`  | Borders and dividers on blue         |

- No other white opacities. Secondary text below .85 fails contrast.
- Never on white, soft or yellow surfaces.

### Green tints
- `green-08` and `green-25` (see `green-success`) inside success components
  only. No other green derivations.

## 4. Approved pairings

| Foreground      | Background      | Ratio   | Verdict                          |
|-----------------|-----------------|---------|----------------------------------|
| `navy-ink`      | `surface-white` | 17.28:1 | Body and headline text           |
| `navy-ink`      | `surface-soft`  | 16.10:1 | Body and headline text           |
| `surface-white` | `blue-base`     | 6.58:1  | Text on blue                     |
| `blue-base`     | `surface-white` | 6.58:1  | Links, CTAs, icons               |
| `red-accent`    | `surface-white` | 5.97:1  | Emphasised word, alert dot       |
| `grey-500`      | `surface-white` | 3.06:1  | Large text, dividers, icons only |
| `navy-ink`      | `yellow-cta`    | 11.05:1 | Button labels on yellow          |
| `yellow-cta`    | `blue-base`     | 4.21:1  | Large accent text, buttons on blue |
| `navy-ink`      | `blue-base`     | 2.63:1  | **Prohibited**                   |
| `red-accent`    | `blue-base`     | 1.10:1  | **Prohibited**                   |
| `green-success` | `surface-white` | 4.17:1  | Ticks, icons, large text only    |
| `green-success` | `blue-base`     | 1.58:1  | **Prohibited**                   |
| `surface-white` | `yellow-cta`    | 1.56:1  | **Prohibited**                   |
| `yellow-cta`    | `surface-white` | 1.56:1  | **Prohibited** (as text)         |

## 5. Proportions on a typical canvas

- `surface-white` / `surface-soft`: most of the canvas.
- `blue-base`: the dominant colour — hero blocks, CTAs, key graphics, or the
  full background of a blue slide/post.
- `navy-ink`: all text on light surfaces.
- `red-accent`: under ~5%.
- `yellow-cta`: the CTA button plus at most one accent on blue; under ~5%.
- `green-success`: functional marks only; under ~5%.
- `grey-500`: structural details only.
